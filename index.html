HOW TO PLAY

MATCH RULES

  • Each tank has 3 HP per round.
  • Destroying the opposing tank wins one round.
  • First to 3 round wins takes the best-of-five match.
  • Tanks cannot cross the center divider. Projectiles can.
  • Every round begins with an arena-overlay 3 → 2 → 1 → GO countdown.

PC CONTROLS

  • WASD / Arrow Keys: move.
  • Mouse: aim the turret.
  • Hold Left Mouse: fire whenever the cooldown is ready.
  • P: pause single-player only.

Phone build: left stick moves; the right aim stick fires automatically only while that stick is actively being used.

POWER-UPS

Power-ups do not add status text during combat. Learn the symbols here; in the arena they simply take effect when collected.

SPEED

Increases movement speed by 35% for 6 seconds.

RAPID FIRE
